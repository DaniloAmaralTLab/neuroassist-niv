## Contexto

Each business service authors its DDL under services/<svc>/src/main/resources/db/migration/. MIG must be the sole Flyway runner (INV-MIG-7) and must apply all scripts from one deterministic, inspectable classpath location. A Gradle aggregation task at build time copies each module's reserved-block scripts (routed by their `-- schema:` annotation) into two MIG classpath locations: db/migration/global and db/migration/tenant. The flat V1__create_initial_schema.sql that the scaffold generates is explicitly excluded (ADR-003). This resolves integration-contracts.md G2.

## Escopo
