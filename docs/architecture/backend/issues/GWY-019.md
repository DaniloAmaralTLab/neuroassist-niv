## Contexto

Declares the `MODULE` constant used by logging and tracing to identify the service
("gateway"), and enforces package-layer constraints with ArchUnit. For a gateway service
the layer model differs from a standard hexagonal service: the packages are `config`,
`filter`, and `error` (no `domain`, `application`, `infrastructure` business layers).
ArchUnit rules here are lighter: no Spring MVC `@RestController` annotation in `filter/`
or `error/`, no business domain class (no JPA entities, no repositories), and the
`MODULE` constant must be resolvable. Apply the `ADR-001-hexagonal-architecture.md`
principle where relevant (stateless filters, no shared mutable state between exchanges).
