## Contexto
Declare all 12 `trn.*` authority constant strings in a single `RoleAuthorityPolicy` class. These constants are referenced by every `@PreAuthorize` annotation in the controllers (D-642) and by the V0089 migration. Without them, `@PreAuthorize` strings can diverge from seeded authority codes. `@EnableMethodSecurity(prePostEnabled=true)` must be present in `SecurityConfig`; without it `@PreAuthorize` is silently inert (D-642).
