Design System — v1.0 · 2026-07-27
Aesthetic direction

"Silent guardian" — light theme, calm and discreet most of the time. The alarm color (red) is deliberately absent from the brand palette and reserved exclusively for the HELP_REQUESTED event — never used for a generic form error. Inspiration: Apple Health · home alarm apps (discreet normal state, unmistakable alert).
